# Code of Conduct

Foil Engine follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, criticize ideas rather than people, and no harassment of any kind is tolerated, in issues, PRs, discussions or in game.

To report behavior: use GitHub's private reporting, or contact the organization's maintainers.
